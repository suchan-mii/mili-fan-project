Mili

Japanese/Tokyo-based indie classical ensemble

Formed 2012 · Active

About (click to toggle)

Mili is a Japanese indie music group known for their unique blend of classical, electronic, and progressive sounds. Their music features intricate compositions, ethereal vocals, and deeply emotional storytelling. They are widely recognized for their contributions to game soundtracks, including Library of Ruina, Limbus Company, and various rhythm games.
